# Module 2: Prompt Engineering

## What is a Prompt?

A prompt is the input you give to an AI model. Better prompts = better results. This is the most important skill for working with AI.

## The 4 Elements of Great Prompts

### 1. Role
Tell the AI WHO to be.

❌ Bad: "Write code"
✅ Good: "You are a senior Python developer with 10 years of experience. Write clean, well-documented code."

### 2. Context
Give background information.

❌ Bad: "Explain loops"
✅ Good: "I'm a beginner learning Python in Ethiopia. Explain for loops with simple examples I can relate to."

### 3. Task
Be SPECIFIC about what you want.

❌ Bad: "Help me with my project"
✅ Good: "Write a Python function that takes a list of Ethiopian cities and returns them sorted by population"

### 4. Format
Specify HOW you want the output.

❌ Bad: "Tell me about AI"
✅ Good: "List 5 applications of AI in Ethiopia. For each, write: (1) the sector, (2) what the AI does, (3) who benefits. Use bullet points."

## Advanced Techniques

### Chain of Thought
Ask the AI to think step by step.

"Solve this problem step by step. First, identify what we know. Second, determine what we need to find. Third, show the calculation. Finally, give the answer."

### Few-Shot Learning
Give examples of what you want first.

"Translate to Amharic:
Hello → ሰላም
Goodbye → ደህና ሁን
Thank you → አመሰግናለሁ
Good morning →"

### Zero-Shot
Just ask directly (no examples).

"What is the capital of Ethiopia?"

## Common Prompt Patterns

| Pattern | When to Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Summarize | Long text needs condensing | "Summarize this article in 3 bullet points" |
| Extract | Pull specific info | "Extract all email addresses from this text" |
| Classify | Categorize something | "Is this review positive, negative, or neutral?" |
| Generate | Create new content | "Write a cover letter for a Python developer job" |
| Translate | Convert between languages | "Translate this to Amharic" |
| Explain | Understand a concept | "Explain neural networks like I'm 5 years old" |

## Ethiopian Context Tips

- Use Amharic naturally — the AI understands code-switching
- Reference local examples (Ethiopian Airlines, Ethio Telecom, coffee)
- Consider cultural norms in your prompts
